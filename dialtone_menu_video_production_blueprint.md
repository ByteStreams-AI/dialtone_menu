# DialTone.Menu Ad Campaign Production Blueprint

This blueprint outlines a 3-part video ad campaign utilizing a lean, budget-friendly AI face-swap workflow. It stars a recurring brand anchor (The Maître D') to showcase the core value propositions of **DialTone.Menu**.

---

## 🛠️ The Production Workflow (Face-Swap Strategy)

To achieve perfect character consistency across completely different scenes without a real actor or green screen, follow this sequence:

1. **The Master Portrait:** Use **[DaVinci AI](https://davinci.ai/)** to generate a single, high-resolution portrait of your ideal Maître D' character. Use a clear, front-facing lighting prompt. This single image serves as your **Source Face**.
2. **Base Video Generation:** Generate the base video clips using the text-to-video prompts below. Look for generated actors that share a similar face shape and hair color to your master portrait to ensure a flawless merge.
3. **The Face Swap:** Take your generated video clips and run them through a video face-swapping pipeline to map your Master Portrait onto the moving video character frame-by-frame.
4. **Post-Production:** Drop the face-swapped video clips into **DaVinci Resolve**, layer your audio tracks, and add your text overlays.

## 📹 Video Ad 1: The "Missing Calls" Pain Point (Campaign Opener)

### Visual Prompts for DaVinci AI

*   **Prompt 1 (The Stressed Maître D'):**

  > Cinematic medium shot, a charismatic male maître d' in a sharp dark suit standing at a restaurant podium. He looks completely exhausted and frazzled, holding an old-school ringing desk phone in one hand. Multiple flashing lights on a wall phone panel behind him. Crisp commercial lighting, shallow depth of field.
* **Prompt 2 (The Empty Spindle):**

  > Extreme close-up of a metal kitchen order spindle, completely empty and bare, standing on a stainless steel counter. Soft, dramatic background blur of an inactive kitchen corner.
* **Prompt 3 (The Relief):**

  > Medium shot of the same maître d' putting the phone down with a sigh of relief, looking directly at the camera with a calm, reassured smile. Warm, professional restaurant ambient lighting.

### Video Script & Audio Blueprint

* **[Audio FX]:** *Overlapping, obnoxious telephone ringtones. Muffled, chaotic chatter of a busy dining room.*
* **Maître D' (To camera, exasperated):** "Every time that phone rings and nobody answers, we don't just lose a call... we lose a customer."
* **[Visual Cut]:** *Quick flash to the Empty Spindle.*
* **Maître D' (Voiceover):** "Friday night rush, the staff is slammed, and hungry people are hanging up to order from the place down the street. It’s costing us thousands."
* **[Visual Cut]:** *Cut back to the Maître D' looking relaxed.*
* **Maître D' (Smiling):** "Stop letting your revenue go to voicemail. Get **DialTone.Menu**. It automates your phone orders and reservations, so your staff can focus on the floor."
* **[Text on Screen]:** **Never Miss an Order. Start Free at DialTone.Menu**

## 📹 Video Ad 2: The Restaurant Chaos & Great Food

### Visual Prompts for DaVinci AI

* **Prompt 1 (The Direct Address):**

  > Cinematic medium close-up, a charismatic male maître d' in a sharp suit standing at a restaurant podium talking directly to the camera with expressive hand gestures. He looks professional yet visibly stressed. Warm ambient restaurant lighting.
* **Prompt 2 (Kitchen Chaos):**

  > Fast-paced tracking shot through a chaotic, high-end restaurant kitchen. Chefs rushing, steam rising, pans flying, high-energy atmosphere, cinematic motion blur.
* **Prompt 3 (The Satisfied Guest):**

  > Close-up of a restaurant guest taking a bite of an exquisitely plated gourmet dish, closing their eyes in pure delight. Warm, appetizing commercial lighting.

### Video Script & Audio Blueprint

* **[Audio FX]:** *Loud phone ringing repeatedly, muffled yells of "Order up!" and clanking pans from the kitchen.*
* **Maître D' (Warm but frazzled tone):** "Welcome! Table for two? Perfect. Right this way..."
* **[Audio FX]:** *Another loud phone ring cuts him off.*
* **Maître D':** "Apologies. As you can see, it is absolute chaos back there tonight. The phones won't stop, the kitchen is drowning... there *has* to be a better solution to handle all this."
* **[Audio FX]:** *Chaos abruptly drops in volume; smooth, calm background jazz kicks in.*
* **Maître D' (Smiling, pointing to a plate):** "...But hey, the food is really good. To fix the rest? I think we should get **DialTone.Menu**."

## 📹 Video Ad 3: The Empty Food Truck (The Location Pivot)

### Visual Prompts for DaVinci AI

* **Prompt 1 (The Empty Lot):**

  > Wide shot of a trendy, modern food truck parked in the center of a wide, bright, completely empty asphalt parking lot. Sunny day, cinematic composition. The Maître D' in his sharp suit stands right outside the service window, looking at his wrist watch.
* **Prompt 2 (The Customer Rush):**

  > Time-lapse or fast-paced shot of a massive, energetic line of hungry customers suddenly appearing and lining up in front of the food truck window. High energy, cheerful atmosphere.

### Video Script & Audio Blueprint

* **[Audio FX]:** *Distant sound of birds chirping and a gentle breeze blowing. Completely dead silence.*
* **Maître D' (Standing by the truck, talking to camera):** "We have the best tacos in the city... but if nobody knows we parked here today, we're just cooking for the birds."
* **[Audio FX]:** *A sharp, satisfying text message notification chime.*
* **Maître D':** "That’s why we use **DialTone.Menu**. One click, and every loyalty member within two miles gets an instant text alert. Now..."
* **[Audio FX]:** *Sudden burst of upbeat crowd chatter, laughing, and sizzling grill sounds.*
* **Maître D' (Surrounded by a massive crowd, smiling):** "...we need more cilantro."
