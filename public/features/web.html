Features / Web
Web

Your own ordering website.

Every DialTone restaurant gets a branded ordering website at its own address — your logo, your colors, your live menu, and a real checkout. Guests order from a search result or a link in your bio without installing anything, and the money lands in your Stripe account, not a delivery app's.

It is the same catalog the phone agent reads and the same catalog the kiosk shows. Change a price or 86 an item in the admin and the website changes with everything else — there is no second menu to keep in sync.

  • Your own branded address, not a listing on someone else's marketplace
  • Live menu shared with voice, app, kiosk, and counter
  • Card payment on your own Stripe account — no commission per order
  • Nothing to install: it opens in the guest's browser

Ordering that finishes the job.

A guest builds a cart with the same modifiers and pricing rules every other channel enforces, picks pickup, dine-in, or delivery, and pays by card. The ticket lands in the kitchen the moment the payment clears — the same queue the phone agent and the counter feed.

  • Full modifier groups, priced by your live menu
  • Pickup, dine-in, and delivery handled in one cart
  • Secure card checkout, settled to your own account
  • Paid orders drop straight onto the kitchen display

Built to be found.

The site publishes structured restaurant data — your name, address, hours, phone, and a link to the menu — in the format search engines read, and it ships its own sitemap. It is a real web presence that can rank, not a page hidden behind an app download.

  • Structured data for name, address, hours, and phone
  • Its own sitemap, so the menu is crawlable
  • Fast, mobile-first pages served from the edge
  • Links cleanly from your social profiles and Google listing

Themed to your restaurant.

Pick a layout that suits the room. A photo-forward grid sells the food; a quieter typographic list suits a menu that reads better than it photographs. Your colors, fonts, logo, and hero image carry across whichever you choose, and item photos fall back to a clean branded placeholder rather than a broken frame.

  • Multiple built-in layouts, switched from the admin
  • Your colors, fonts, logo, and hero image throughout
  • Per-item photos, with a branded fallback when one is missing
  • Category jump and search on longer menus
