# DialTone Social — Manual Launch Pack

**Purpose:** get content flowing **now**, by hand, while the automation (PRD-DialTone-Social) is built.
**Posts:** 10, covering ~2 weeks at 5–6/week.
**Consent required:** none. Every asset is either ByteStreams-produced or seeded demo data (Sui's Sushi / Rival Ramen).
**Accounts:** [@dialtone.menu](https://www.instagram.com/dialtone.menu/) · [DialTone Menu on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61591570856435)

---

## Rules applied to every caption below

- **CTA is "Book a 15-minute call."** **There is no free trial, and there will not be one.** The pilot program is closed — Shortys and Diner On The Go were it. Never write "free trial", "try it free", "start free", or "no credit card required" in any post.
- **The video blueprint's outro is wrong.** `dialtone_menu_video_production_blueprint.md` ends every spot with *"Start Your Free Trial at DialTone.Menu"*. That line is now false and must be re-cut or overlaid before any of those spots publish. The three commercials in this pack (#2, #3, #4) — **check their final 5 seconds before posting.**
- **Instagram:** CTA reads "Link in bio." **Facebook:** paste the actual URL — links are clickable there.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5, Instagram only, anchor pair `#DialTone #RestaurantOwners` always. **Zero hashtags on Facebook.**
- **No city tags.** The motion is national.
- **Banned:** "agentic", "AI-powered", "revolutionary", "seamless", "leverage", "restaurant tech". ("AI agent" / "voice agent" in body copy is fine.)
- **Outcome first, feature name second.**
- **Facebook variant:** same idea, longer, no hashtags, real link. Noted per post where it differs meaningfully.

## 7 — Operator education: build the menu for speed

**Format:** Carousel (4 cards, text) · **Asset:** brand cards — make in Canva at 1080×1350
**Pillar:** Operator education — **no product mention at all**

> Food truck profitability, the part nobody tells you:
>
> At a weekday lunch spot, speed *is* the business. Serving 45 in ninety minutes beats serving 30 — and every person who walks off because the line isn't moving was already sold.
>
> Four to seven items. Pre-cook what you can. Assemble to order, don't cook to order.
>
> Festival or a game is a different animal — there you can afford three or four minutes and a menu worth driving for. Different spot, different menu.

`#DialTone #RestaurantOwners #FoodTruckLife #FoodTruckOwner`

**Card copy:** (1) "Speed is the menu decision." (2) "4–7 items at a lunch spot." (3) "Assemble to order. Don't cook to order." (4) "Festivals are a different menu."
